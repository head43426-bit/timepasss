How it works

Four steps, your logs only

01

Upload a .log file in the workspace.

02

ASK detects and labels it — MITRE + kill-chain stage.

03

Steps link into incidents, worst risk first.

04

Read the report, graph and what to block first.

Honest limit: ASK reads logs. It has no agent on your hosts and no firewall control, so it cannot actively block — protection is concrete per-technique actions, not a promise to stop every attack. Use only synthetic or authorised logs, never real production logs or credentials.
